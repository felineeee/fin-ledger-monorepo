## 3. Phase 3: Inter-Branch Transfers

### Transfer Management

- `[ ] GET /api/transfers` — List stock movement requests
- `[ ] GET /api/transfers/:id` — View specific transfer manifest and item breakdown
- `[ ] GET /api/locations/:id/transfers/incoming` — Filtered view: transfers destined for this location
- `[ ] GET /api/locations/:id/transfers/outgoing` — Filtered view: transfers originating from this location
- `[ ] POST /api/transfers` — Submit a transfer request between locations
- `[ ] PATCH /api/transfers/:id/cancel` — Cancel a transfer while status is `PENDING` (before dispatch)

### Dispatch & Receive Flow

- `[ ] POST /api/transfers/:id/dispatch` — Deduct stock from origin `inventory_levels`, write `TRANSFER_OUT` to ledger, and update status to `IN_TRANSIT`
- `[ ] POST /api/transfers/:id/receive` — Increment stock at destination `inventory_levels`, write `TRANSFER_IN` to ledger, and complete transfer
- `[ ] POST /api/transfers/:id/reject` — Destination location refuses damaged or wrong shipment (routes stock to quarantine or resolution path)

## 4. Phase 4: Stocktakes & Cycle Counting

### Stocktake Management

- `[ ] GET /api/stocktakes` — List active and historical counts
- `[ ] GET /api/stocktakes/:id` — Fetch stocktake status and list of snapshot items to count
- `[ ] POST /api/stocktakes` — Start a new count session, taking a snapshot of `inventory_levels` to populate `expected_quantity`
- `[ ] DELETE /api/stocktakes/:id` — Cancel/abort an `IN_PROGRESS` stocktake session (e.g. operator error)

### Counting & Reconciliation

- `[ ] POST /api/stocktakes/:id/count` — Submit batch barcode scans to update `counted_quantity`
- `[ ] PATCH /api/stocktakes/:id/count/:itemId` — Correct a single miscounted item quantity before finalizing
- `[ ] GET /api/stocktakes/:id/variance-report` — Review expected vs. counted discrepancies before committing adjustments
- `[ ] POST /api/stocktakes/:id/complete` — Lock stocktake, calculate variances, update `inventory_levels` to match reality, and write `ADJUSTMENT` / `SHRINKAGE` to `inventory_ledger`

## 5. Phase 5: Reverse Logistics & Quarantine

### Returns & Disposition

- `[ ] POST /api/returns` — Process customer return into virtual quarantine location (writes RETURN to ledger)
- `[ ] POST /api/returns/:id/restock` — Move pristine item from quarantine back to sales floor
- `[ ] POST /api/returns/:id/discard` — Write off damaged item from quarantine (writes DAMAGE to ledger)
