## 1. Phase 1: Logistics Foundation (Core Inventory)

### Locations

- `[ ] GET /api/locations` — List all stores, warehouses, and virtual zones
- `[ ] POST /api/locations` — Create a new location
- `[ ] PATCH /api/locations/:id` — Update location details or deactivate a location

### Inventory & Ledger

- `[ ] GET /api/locations/:id/inventory` — Fetch current `inventory_levels` for a specific store
- `[ ] GET /api/products/:id/inventory` — View stock level breakdown across all locations for a specific product
- `[ ] POST /api/inventory/adjust` — Perform manual stock adjustments; updates `inventory_levels` and writes `SHRINKAGE` record to `inventory_ledger`
- `[ ] GET /api/inventory/ledger` — Query audit trail filterable by product, location, or date

---

## 2. Phase 2: Purchase Orders (Supplier Procurement)

### Suppliers

- `[ ] GET /api/suppliers` — List all suppliers and contact details
- `[ ] POST /api/suppliers` — Register a new supplier

### Purchase Orders

- `[ ] GET /api/purchase-orders` — List POs with optional filtering by status
- `[ ] GET /api/purchase-orders/:id` — View full PO details and nested line items
- `[ ] POST /api/purchase-orders` — Create a `DRAFT` PO with requested items and quantities
- `[ ] PATCH /api/purchase-orders/:id/status` — Update state of PO

### The Receiving Engine

- `[ ] POST /api/purchase-orders/:id/receive` — Process inbound scanned items, update `quantity_received`, increment `inventory_levels`, and write `RECEIPT` to `inventory_ledger`

---

## 3. Phase 3: Inter-Branch Transfers

### Transfer Management

- `[ ] GET /api/transfers` — List stock movement requests
- `[ ] GET /api/transfers/:id` — View specific transfer manifest and item breakdown
- `[ ] POST /api/transfers` — Submit a transfer request between locations

### Dispatch & Receive Flow

- `[ ] POST /api/transfers/:id/dispatch` — Deduct stock from origin `inventory_levels`, write `TRANSFER_OUT` to ledger, and update status to `IN_TRANSIT`
- `[ ] POST /api/transfers/:id/receive` — Increment stock at destination `inventory_levels`, write `TRANSFER_IN` to ledger, and complete transfer

---

## 4. Phase 4: Stocktakes & Cycle Counting

### Stocktake Management

- `[ ] GET /api/stocktakes` — List active and historical counts
- `[ ] POST /api/stocktakes` — Start a new count session, taking a snapshot of `inventory_levels` to populate `expected_quantity`
- `[ ] GET /api/stocktakes/:id` — Fetch list of items to count

### Counting & Reconciliation

- `[ ] POST /api/stocktakes/:id/count` — Submit batch barcode scans to update `counted_quantity`
- `[ ] POST /api/stocktakes/:id/complete` — Lock stocktake, calculate variances, update `inventory_levels` to match reality, and write `ADJUSTMENT` to `inventory_ledger`
