## 1. Warehouse Domain Lifecycle

A warehouse operates through 5 distinct physical stages:

- **Commissioning (`POST /locations`)** — Registers a new physical location (`type: 'WAREHOUSE'`). Inventory levels start at zero.
- **Inbound Procurement (`POST /purchase-orders/:id/receive`)** — Goods arrive from external suppliers. Creates/updates `inventory_levels` and writes a `RECEIPT` transaction to `inventory_ledger`.
- **Outbound Distribution (`POST /transfers/:id/dispatch`)** — Sends stock to retail stores. Decrements warehouse `inventory_levels` and records a `TRANSFER_OUT` ledger entry.
- **Auditing & Maintenance (`POST /stocktakes`)** — Adjusts for physical discrepancies, damage, or theft via `SHRINKAGE` or `ADJUSTMENT` ledger transactions.
- **Decommissioning (`PATCH /locations/:id`)** — Stock is drained to zero via transfers, and `is_active` is set to `false`. The location remains soft-locked in the database for historical reporting.

## 2. Product Lifecycle (Fungible Inventory)

A specific variant (e.g., Medium Black T-Shirt) travels through 5 transactional states:

1. **Anticipation (PO Created):** Promised stock logged in `purchase_order_items`. Ledger unchanged.
2. **Birth (PO Received):** Stock enters the financial ecosystem. `inventory_levels` increases; `RECEIPT` ledger entry created (+100).
3. **Journey (Transfer):** Dispatched from warehouse (`TRANSFER_OUT`, -10) and received at store (`TRANSFER_IN`, +10). While in transit, stock exists only inside `transfer_items`.
4. **Reservation (Soft Allocation):** Online cart checkout sets `quantity_reserved: +1`. `quantity_on_hand` stays unchanged, but `available` stock drops (`on_hand - reserved`).
5. **Exit (Fulfilled Sale):** Item shipped. `quantity_reserved` (-1) and `quantity_on_hand` (-1) drop; `SALE` ledger entry logged (-1).

## 3. Reverse Logistics Lifecycle (Returns)

To prevent compromised or damaged returns from inflating buyable stock, returned items route through a **Virtual Quarantine Location**:

1. **Intake (`POST /returns`):**

- Customer returns item.
- Stock is assigned to a virtual location (`type: 'VIRTUAL'`) with a `RETURN` ledger entry (+1).
- Website stock remains unchanged so damaged goods aren't sold online.

2. **Inspection & Resolution:**

- **Pristine Condition:** Transferred from the virtual bin back to the sales floor via `TRANSFER_OUT` (bin) and `TRANSFER_IN` (store). Re-enters sellable inventory.
- **Damaged Condition:** Written off via `POST /inventory/adjust` with a `DAMAGE` ledger entry (-1) to record the financial loss.
