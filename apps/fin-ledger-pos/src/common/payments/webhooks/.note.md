Here is the list of fields to configure in your Xendit Dashboard, organized in a table.

For every row marked with **FILL**, paste your webhook endpoint URL:

- **Local Development (via ngrok):** `[https://your-ngrok-id.ngrok-free.app/api/webhooks/gateway](https://your-ngrok-id.ngrok-free.app/api/webhooks/gateway)`
- **Production:** `[https://api.yourdomain.com/api/webhooks/gateway](https://api.yourdomain.com/api/webhooks/gateway)`
