### 1. Inbound Stock (Increases Inventory)

- `RECEIPT` / `PURCHASE`: Stock received from a vendor or purchase order.
- `RETURN_CUSTOMER`: Stock brought back by a customer and put back into sellable condition.
- `MANUFACTURE_IN`: Finished goods produced internally and added to available stock.

---

### 2. Outbound Stock (Decreases Inventory)

- `SALE` / `FULFILLMENT`: Stock deducted when an order is sold or shipped to a customer.
- `RETURN_VENDOR`: Defective or unsold stock shipped back to a supplier.
- `MANUFACTURE_OUT` / `CONSUMPTION`: Raw materials consumed during a manufacturing process.

---

### 3. Inventory Adjustments & Audit Deltas

- `SHRINKAGE`: Unexplained loss due to theft, miscounting, or administrative errors.
- `DAMAGE` / `SPOILAGE` / `SCRAP`: Stock written off because it is broken, expired, or unusable.
- `CYCLE_COUNT` / `RECONCILIATION`: Manual adjustments made after physical inventory counts.

---

### 4. Transfers & Relocations

- `TRANSFER_OUT`: Stock departing a branch/warehouse en route to another location.
- `TRANSFER_IN`: Stock arriving at a destination branch/warehouse from a transfer.
- `TRANSFER_IN_TRANSIT`: Stock currently moving between locations (reduces source, but not yet received at target).
